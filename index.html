CYBER SHIELD · OVERVIEW

Understand the risk. Choose a safer next step.

Cyber Shield was created to make everyday cyber risks easier to recognize and respond to. It brings browser protection, practical checks and guided learning into one connected platform.

MADE FOR EVERYDAY SAFETY
01

Why we built Cyber Shield

People make security decisions every day, often without clear context or useful guidance. A convincing link, a hidden QR destination or a weak website setting can turn a routine action into a security incident.

Deceptive links

Lookalike addresses and unsafe destinations can trick people into exposing accounts or downloading harmful files.

Messages and QR codes

Urgent messages and concealed QR destinations can hide scams in ordinary communication.

Hard-to-see security gaps

Website settings and unfamiliar vulnerability patterns are difficult to assess without clear, practical explanations.

02

How the platform helps

Each part addresses a different step: notice a risk, understand what it means, and learn how safer systems are built.

Chrome Extension

Checks browsing links and can warn about or block destinations identified as harmful.

Smart Scan

Website Scan

WEBSITE REVIEW

Cryptology Lab

LEARN BY DOING

Simulation Lab

Study common web vulnerability classes in isolated demonstrations, compare vulnerable and safer patterns, and review mitigations.

SAFE PRACTICE
03

Our goal

Our goal is to make cybersecurity clearer, more practical and easier to use. We want people to recognize warning signs before they act, understand why a finding matters, and know what safer next step they can take.

Earlier awareness

Help users notice suspicious links, messages and website signals before they become harmful actions.

Clearer decisions

Explain what a result means, what its limits are, and which practical response is appropriate.

Safer learning

Build confidence through guided cryptology and isolated security simulations that connect concepts with real defensive habits.
